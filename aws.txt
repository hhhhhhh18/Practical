AWS Global Infrastructure

AWS Global Infrastructure consists of:

1. Regions
2. Availability Zones
3. Edge Locations
4. Points of Presence

Regions:
An AWS Region is a separate geographical area containing multiple Availability Zones.

Availability Zones:
Availability Zones are isolated data centers within an AWS Region.

Edge Locations:
Edge Locations are used by services such as Amazon CloudFront to deliver content closer to users.

Points of Presence:
Points of Presence are locations used by AWS services for low-latency content delivery and networking.
